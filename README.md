## Featured project

### [Rivalscope](https://rivalscope.dev) - competitive intelligence for SaaS teams

Give it a competitor's URL and it tracks their homepage for changes, aggregates customer complaints from G2 and Trustpilot, watches for hiring and strategic signals, and turns it all into a ranked sales playbook. The core is an automated scan pipeline that diffs competitor pages, classifies each change, and feeds an AI Battle Card.

- Stack: FastAPI, Next.js, TypeScript, DeepSeek, PostgreSQL, Railway, Vercel
- Status: Live at [rivalscope.dev](https://rivalscope.dev), roughly 3,000 signups and its first paying customers
